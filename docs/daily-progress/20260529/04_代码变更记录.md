## 5. Delivery 路由

### `src/routes/delivery.routes.js`

- 新增三尺寸交付 API：

```text
POST /api/delivery/scan
POST /api/delivery/start
POST /api/delivery/resume
POST /api/delivery/stop
POST /api/delivery/runs/:runId/retry-target
POST /api/delivery/runs/:runId/select-candidate
POST /api/delivery/runs/:runId/standardize
POST /api/delivery/runs/:runId/finalize
GET  /api/delivery/status
GET  /api/delivery/runs
GET  /api/delivery/runs/:runId
GET  /api/delivery/image
```

- `delivery-candidates` 任务接入 `automationState.legilTaskProgress`。
- 每个 target 独立记录：
  - `pending`
  - `generating`
  - `candidates_ready`
  - `candidate_selected`
  - `standardized`
  - `failed`
- 支持停止、继续、重跑单 target。
- 支持标准化选中候选。

### `src/server/context.js`
